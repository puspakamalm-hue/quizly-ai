Frequently Asked Questions

What is Quizly AI?

Quizly AI is an online AI quiz maker that creates interactive multiple-choice quizzes from a topic you provide.

Is Quizly AI free?

The Quizly AI website is designed to be free to use. AI generation may be subject to service limits and API costs.

What topics can I use?

You can enter topics such as science, history, geography, technology, animals, mathematics, sports and general knowledge.

How are the questions generated?

Quizly AI sends your quiz topic, difficulty and question count to its secure AI backend, which uses the OpenAI API to generate the quiz.
