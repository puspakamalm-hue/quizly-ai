Frequently Asked Questions About Quizly AI

Answers to common questions about our free AI quiz maker.

Is Quizly AI free?
Yes. Quizly AI is designed as a free online AI quiz maker that lets you create quizzes and test your knowledge without creating questions manually.
What is an AI quiz generator?
An AI quiz generator uses artificial intelligence to create quiz questions from a topic or subject that you provide. Quizly AI generates multiple-choice questions based on your selected topic and difficulty.
Can I create a quiz on any topic?
You can enter many different subjects and topics, including science, mathematics, history, geography, technology, literature, general knowledge, and more. For important academic topics, review AI-generated answers against trusted learning resources.
How many questions can I generate?
Quizly AI currently lets you choose between 5, 10, and 15 questions for each generated quiz.
Can I choose the quiz difficulty?
Yes. You can choose Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty before generating your quiz.
Is Quizly AI useful for students?
Yes. Students can use it for revision, self-testing, practice questions, and checking their understanding of a topic.
Can teachers use Quizly AI?
Teachers and tutors can use Quizly AI to generate question ideas and practice activities. Important educational material should be reviewed before being used in formal assessments.
Does Quizly AI show my quiz score?
Yes. After completing a quiz, Quizly AI shows your percentage score, correct answers, incorrect answers, total questions, and an answer review.
Can I download my quiz results?
Yes. After completing a quiz, you can use the Download Results button to save a text copy of your results and answer review.
Does Quizly AI use artificial intelligence?
Yes. Quizly AI uses an AI-powered backend to generate multiple-choice quiz questions based on the topic, number of questions, and difficulty you select.
